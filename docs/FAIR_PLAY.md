# Fair-play policy

Owner: spec. Public text (published on the website and in Settings › About & legal). Enforced in the content
catalogue (ADR 0016) and moderation (ADR 0019).

## Our promise

Rustic is a launcher for playing fairly with friends. We promote content that makes the game nicer to play, look at
and share, and never content that gives you an unfair advantage over other players.

## What we never list, promote or bundle

- **X-ray and see-through-blocks** tools, ore finders, cave finders, entity radars that reveal hidden players or mobs
  through walls, "ESP" overlays, tracers.
- **Automation of combat or input**: auto-clickers, kill-aura, aim assist, auto-totem, trigger bots, macros that click or
  attack faster than a person can, reach or hitbox changes, velocity/knockback changes.
- **Movement cheats**: fly, speed, no-fall, step, jesus/water-walk, scaffold, freecam used to see hidden areas on
  multiplayer servers.
- **Exploits and griefing tools**: dupes, crash or lag exploits, packet manipulation, server crashers, account or
  session stealers, anything that hides itself from server anticheats.
- Any mod that sends a player's account, token, or personal data to third parties.

## What is fine

Performance improvements, shaders and resource packs, HUD information that the game already gives you (coordinates
where the server allows them, FPS, keystrokes, armour durability), minimaps that show only what you have explored and
that servers can disable, zoom, cosmetics (purely visual, ours never affect gameplay), accessibility tools.

Some features are fine in single player but not on every server (e.g. minimaps, fullbright). Server rules always win:
if a server forbids something, don't use it there.

## Cosmetics

Rustic cosmetics are purely visual. They never change hitboxes, visibility, sounds, speed or any game mechanic. No capes
or cape-like items, no random draws, no loot boxes.

## Servers created with Rustic

Server owners choose their own rules and plugins. Plugins from the Rustic catalogue declare their capabilities; we
remove plugins that harm players (data harvesting, backdoors, scams).

## Reporting and enforcement

- Report content in the launcher (Content › item › Report) or a player (friends panel / profile › Report).
- Content published on the Rustic marketplace is checked against this policy when it is uploaded (names,
  tags and categories that name a cheat are refused; single-player-only features are published with a notice
  and never promoted). Native mods are read by a person before they are public. Every project page has a
  Report button.
- Content that breaks this policy is removed from the catalogue; a version that hides cheating features is removed
  with all its versions.
- Accounts that distribute cheats through Rustic or abuse other players can be warned, restricted or banned, with a
  statement of reasons and an appeal by email.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the date of the last change is shown at the top of the published page.
